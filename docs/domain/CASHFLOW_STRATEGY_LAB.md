# Cashflow strategy lab - what thousands of simulated games show

_Generated by `node scripts/strategy-lab.js` - do not edit by hand. Rules version `080250730e9970`, 300 games for every profession and strategy (seeds 1000-1299), at most 400 rolls a game. The same seeds are used for every strategy, so the strategies are compared on the same dice._

A game is **won** when passive income covers every monthly expense (escaping the rat race) and **lost** when the monthly cashflow turns negative (bankruptcy). A game that has not ended after the roll limit is a timeout. "Turns" are rolls; "months" are Paydays.

- **Never invests** (`never-buy`): Pays every Doodad, passes every deal. The salary alone: what the rat race is without investing.
- **Properties from cash** (`cash-properties`): Buys any property it can pay the deposit for out of cash - no loans, no reserve, small deals only.
- **Picky properties** (`picky-properties`): Only properties returning at least 3% a month on the deposit, with a 2.000 reserve kept; small deals.
- **Small first, Big when rich** (`big-deals-when-rich`): Draws Small Deals until cash reaches 20.000, then Big Deals; buys any property the cash pays for, keeps 1.000.
- **Borrow when it pays** (`bridge-loans`): Borrows the shortfall when a property pays at least 1,5 times the loan interest it adds; repays when it can.
- **Leveraged, Big Deals** (`leveraged-big`): Aims at Big Deals from the start and borrows whenever the property pays at least the interest.
- **Marketing first, then properties** (`mlm-then-properties`): Buys every Multi-Level-Marketing card it can afford (the best card on average), then properties from cash.
- **Trade stocks for deposits** (`stocks-for-deposits`): Buys stocks at 10 or less and sells at 25 or more to raise deposit money, then buys properties.
- **All-rounder** (`all-rounder`): Properties above 2% a month with a reserve, loans only when well covered, Multi-Level-Marketing, stock trading, selling to generous buyers, Big Deals once cash allows.
- **Best strategy found by the search** (`best-found`): properties paying at least 4% a month, no reserve, borrows when the card pays for it, Big Deals from 20,000 cash, buys Multi-Level-Marketing

## The search for a better strategy

216 combinations of the strategy knobs were each played 40 times on hausmeister, lehrer, manager, pilot, arzt. The best five:

- 93% escape, 7% bankrupt, median 70 rolls: properties paying at least 4% a month, no reserve, borrows when the card pays for it, Big Deals from 20,000 cash, buys Multi-Level-Marketing
- 93% escape, 7% bankrupt, median 71 rolls: any property, no reserve, borrows when the card pays for it, Big Deals from 20,000 cash, buys Multi-Level-Marketing
- 93% escape, 7% bankrupt, median 71 rolls: properties paying at least 2% a month, no reserve, borrows when the card pays for it, Big Deals from 20,000 cash, buys Multi-Level-Marketing
- 93% escape, 7% bankrupt, median 145 rolls: any property, no reserve, borrows when the card pays for it, Small Deals only, buys Multi-Level-Marketing
- 93% escape, 7% bankrupt, median 145 rolls: properties paying at least 2% a month, no reserve, borrows when the card pays for it, Small Deals only, buys Multi-Level-Marketing

"Best" means best among what was tried, not proven best.
